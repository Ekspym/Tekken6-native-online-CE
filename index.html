About this update

These are the balance changes made so far for the Community Edition. The list is built from the current balance file, so it shows exactly what the game plays.

This mod was made to raise the overall quality of Tekken 6 and breathe new life into its cast. Characters who were too dominant have been toned down so they feel less oppressive, while characters who were underperforming have been made stronger or simply more fun to play.

The goal of this patch is not a perfectly balanced game. The goal is a more enjoyable one.

All frame values are measured in game frames, the same way the practice frame data display counts them. Frame advantage is from the attacker's side.

System

  • Practice: the frame data display now shows the real frame advantage, also for moves with their own timing.
